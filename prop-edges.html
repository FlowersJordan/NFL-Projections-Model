Edge = our projection − the book's line (positive favors the over). Standard is the book's main two-way line. Alternate lines are the other rungs of its ladder (usually one-sided “X+” prices): a far rung always sits far from our number, so its z mostly reflects distance, not value. Odds are for the side we lean toward; “—” means the book doesn't offer that side (typically an Under on an alternate). Rows with a play probability under 70% are dimmed.
