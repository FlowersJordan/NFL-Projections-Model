A description, not a forecast. Comps are matched on size, testing and how each player produced and was used in his final college season. In a walk-forward test, athletic comps ranked careers poorly (rank correlation 0.13–0.22), college comps somewhat better (0.23–0.35), and draft position far better (0.61–0.69). Adding college production to draft position in a model did not improve it. Use comps to see what kind of player a rookie is; draft slot should drive expectations.
