Cushion = distance from the line to his worst (floors) or best (ceilings) game. L17 = how often he cleared that line in his last 17 games, across seasons. Implied = the win probability the price needs (includes the book’s margin). EV = (L17 rate smoothed as (wins+1)/(games+2) × payout) − 1.
